# KCMO Public Innovation Challenge

A reusable specification for running public innovation challenges — AI or otherwise — at
the City of Kansas City, Missouri. The goal is one standard mechanism that any department
can instantiate, and that Procurement can own and operate, instead of redesigning the
process for each challenge.

## The core idea

> **Buy evidence in stages. Increase commitment only after a gate is passed.**

The City defines the service problem, limits its first commitment, evaluates evidence at
defined gates, and expands scope, data access, and funding only after performance is
documented. Stopping is a planned outcome, not a failure.

## Running a new challenge

1. Write **Part 1** for your service problem.
2. Fill the **parameter block** (mechanism spec §1). If a parameter can't be stated, you're not ready.
3. Clear **Stage 0** — baseline measured, ground truth built, data ready, instrument resolved, capacity committed.
4. Execute **Parts 2A and 2B unchanged**.
5. Track the **open-items register** (Appendix A) to closure.

## Authority

This specification is **authority-neutral**. The legal instrument and procurement
authority are set per-instance by the Procurement Division and the Law Department, and
recorded as parameter P1.
